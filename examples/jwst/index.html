wcslib.js Coordinate Translation

WebAssembly-powered WCS coordinate transformations in the browser

About: This demo uses wcslib.js (a JS/WebAssembly transpilation of WCSLIB using emscripten) to convert between pixel coordinates and sky coordinates (RA/Dec) using the WCS information from a JWST NIRCam FITS header. Click on the image to see the sky coordinates, or enter values manually below. The source image is a crop of the F150W band from GOODS-S field from the JADES survey, downloaded from MAST.
